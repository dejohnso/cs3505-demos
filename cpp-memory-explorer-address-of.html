Fake addresses are intentionally simple. Stack begins at 0x1000; heap begins at 0x4000. Raw integers are displayed in decimal. Values that are addresses are displayed in hexadecimal. The address-of operator & is supported, but C++ reference types and pointer-to-pointer types are intentionally not modeled. The heap uses a simple first-fit allocator.
